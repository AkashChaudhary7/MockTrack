Privacy Policy for MockTrack

Exam Score Tracker, Subject Analytics & Performance Center

Effective Date: August 25, 2026 Last Updated: August 25, 2026 Developer Contact: mobographie@gmail.com Canonical URL: https://mocktrack.ictlabgsssaidana.workers.dev/privacy

Summary for Google Play & Candidates: MockTrack is an offline-first, privacy-focused productivity tool. Your mock test scores, target exam selections, mistake journal entries, and study progress are stored 100% locally on your device (using LocalStorage & IndexedDB). We do not require account sign-ups, passwords, or selling of personal data.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the MockTrack application ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"), available as a Progressive Web Application (PWA), Web application, and Trusted Web Activity (TWA) on Google Play.

MockTrack is designed to assist aspirants preparing for Indian competitive exams (such as SSC CGL, Banking/IBPS PO, RRB NTPC, UPSC, NEET, JEE, State PSCs) in tracking their mock test scores, analyzing accuracy, identifying weak subject chapters, and logging revision takeaways.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We believe in strict data minimization. Here is the breakdown of data types:

A. User-Provided Data (Stored Locally On-Device)

  • Candidate Profile Name & Avatar Initial: Used purely for displaying a personalized dashboard greeting (e.g. "Hi, Akash 👋"). Stored exclusively on your device.
  • Mock Test Attempts & Metrics: Test name, platform name (e.g. Testbook, Oliveboard, Coaching, Offline), scores, maximum marks, correct/wrong counts, accuracy percentage, percentiles, ranks, time spent, and question review notes.
  • Exam Profiles & Target Dates: Your selected exam targets (e.g. SSC CGL 2026) and exam countdown dates.

B. Scorecard OCR & Screenshot Scanner

MockTrack offers an optional AI Scorecard Scanner / OCR Feature allowing you to upload a screenshot of your exam test result or submit a test scorecard link to automatically extract marks and sectional breakdowns:

  • When you upload a screenshot or link, the image/data is transmitted over secure encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to our backend parser service solely to execute text recognition and parse marks.
  • No Permanent Image Retention: The image and raw OCR data are discarded immediately after the extracted score values are returned to your local app. We do NOT store, build profiles with, or sell your scorecard images.

C. Automated & Diagnostic Information

  • Offline Caching State: Handled entirely by the browser/PWA Service Worker (sw.js) to allow full offline app performance.
  • No Third-Party Tracking / Ad-Brokers: We do not deploy invasive cross-app ad tracking SDKs or user tracking identifiers.

4. Device Permissions

MockTrack may request standard web/device permissions only when you explicitly invoke specific features:

  • Storage / File Access (Optional): To export JSON/PDF backups to your device or import an existing backup file.
  • Camera / Photo Picker (Optional): To take a photo or select an existing scorecard screenshot from your gallery for AI marks extraction.
  • Notifications (Optional): For PWA exam countdown or revision reminders if explicitly allowed.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion Rights

Because your data resides on your device, you have 100% control over your data at all times:

  • Data Portability / Export: You can export a complete JSON backup file or generate PDF summary reports anytime under Settings > Data Management.
  • Data Erasure: You can permanently wipe all stored mock tests, candidate names, and history by clicking "Clear All Local Mock Data" in the Profile screen or by clearing your browser site data.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MockTrack is intended for students and adult candidates preparing for competitive exams (typically aged 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security protocols:

  • All server communications (PWA assets, OCR processing) occur strictly over encrypted HTTPS with TLS 1.3.
  • Client-side data is sandboxed within your browser/WebView origin storage, preventing unauthorized access by other websites or applications.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to periodically review this page for the latest information.
